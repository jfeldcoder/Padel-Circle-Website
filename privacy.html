Privacy Policy

Last updated · May 11, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Padel Circle ("we", "us") collects, uses, and shares information about you when you use our mobile app and related services. We aim to be transparent and to collect only what we need to operate a great padel community.

Information You Provide

Account data: name, email, phone (optional), date of birth (optional), profile photo, gender, location, skill self-assessment, and preferences. Activity data: match scores, partners, opponents, league entries, tournament registrations, chat messages, dispute submissions, and content you post. Payments: when applicable, billing information is handled by our payment processor; we receive a token and transaction status, not full card numbers.

Information Collected Automatically

Device and usage data: device model, operating system, app version, language, IP address, crash logs, and basic analytics about how features are used. Approximate location may be derived from IP. Precise location is only collected with your explicit permission and only used to surface nearby clubs, leagues, and tournaments.

How We Use Information

We use the information to: (a) provide and improve the Service; (b) authenticate you and secure your account; (c) calculate ratings, standings, and recommendations; (d) deliver notifications you have enabled; (e) prevent fraud and enforce our Terms; (f) comply with legal obligations; and (g) communicate with you about updates, support, and (if you opt in) promotions.

How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information. We share data with: (i) other users, in the form of your public profile, scores, and standings; (ii) operators of leagues or tournaments you join; (iii) service providers (hosting, analytics, push notifications, payments) under contractual data protection obligations; (iv) authorities when required by law or to protect rights and safety.

Push Notifications

If you enable notifications, we use device tokens (via APNs / FCM) to deliver match, league, tournament, and chat alerts. You can disable notifications at any time in Settings or in your device's system settings.

Data Retention

We retain account information for as long as your account is active. Match history and ratings may be retained in anonymized form to preserve historical leaderboards. You can request deletion of your account at any time from Settings → Delete Account, after which we will delete or de-identify your personal data, subject to legal retention requirements.

Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or export your personal information, or to object to or restrict processing. To exercise these rights, email support@padelcircle.app. We will verify your request and respond within the timeframes required by applicable law (e.g., GDPR, CCPA).

Children's Privacy

The Service is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

International Transfers

Your information may be processed in countries other than your own. Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards such as standard contractual clauses for international transfers.

Security

We use industry-standard technical and organizational measures, including encryption in transit, hashed credentials, and role-based access controls. No system is perfectly secure; please use a strong password and contact us if you suspect unauthorized access.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Policy. Material changes will be communicated through the app or by email. The effective date at the top of this document indicates when it was last revised.

Contact

Privacy questions? Email support@padelcircle.app.
